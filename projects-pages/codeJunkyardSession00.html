02

Voxel teaser

The first exciting test is a small voxel system around a curve. Build a 3D grid, take each voxel center, find the closest point on the curve, and keep the voxel when the distance is below a chosen threshold.

This is intentionally a center-distance test, not a full geometric intersection algorithm. It is simple enough for setup day and useful enough to feel like a real design tool.

Rule

Keep voxel if distance(center, curve) <= D.
