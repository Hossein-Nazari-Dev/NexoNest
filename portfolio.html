Professional Summary

I define my work around what I call Computational Climatic Finding — a design approach that integrates climate analysis, data-driven form exploration, and computational workflows to shape architectures, landscapes, and urban systems that are inherently adaptive and environmentally performative.

Alongside this, I explore the potential of Social Digital Twins, creating dynamic simulations of human behavior and comfort within built environments. By merging climatic form-finding with behavioral modeling, my goal is to prototype intelligent design strategies that simultaneously enhance environmental performance and elevate user experience. To me, design is not only a technical pursuit but a framework for sustainable, human-centered transformation.

Selected Projects and Experience

TectoTrack

A social digital twin platform that models how people move, interact, and occupy space in real time. Powered by AI-driven agent-based simulation, it represents diverse behavioral profiles to study crowd dynamics, wayfinding, and environmental comfort—helping designers optimize public spaces, retail layouts, and circulation systems through predictive analytics.

View Project Details

CurvAdapt

A geometry preprocessing framework that bridges free-form NURBS design and simulation accuracy. It provides adaptive segmentation and simplification tools governed by curvature, tangent, and chord-error metrics—ensuring geometries remain both computationally efficient and performance-faithful across daylighting and energy analyses.

View Project Details

ABMs in Architecture and Urban Design

Designing Human-Centered, Environmentally Perceptive Digital Models in Architecture and Urban Planning. This hands-on bootcamp explored digital twins and agent-based models to simulate human behavior in urban spaces. Participants used Rhino, Grasshopper, and Python to tackle real design challenges—like pedestrian flow, emergency evacuation, and spatial perception.
