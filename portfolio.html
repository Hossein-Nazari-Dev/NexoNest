Working Thesis

My work sits between computational climate design and social digital twins. I translate environmental data, behaviour, and geometry into practical design tools for architecture, landscape, and urban systems.

The goal is simple: make complex evidence easier to use early in the design process — before a beautiful idea becomes an expensive mistake.

Selected Work

TectoTrack

A social digital twin and agent-based simulation environment for studying how people move, choose, wait, and use complex buildings. My work spans behaviour logic, routing, queues, accessibility, BIM-to-Unity workflows, and technical system architecture.

View Project Details

CurvAdapt

A reliability-first geometry preprocessing framework for translating NURBS curves into simulation-ready polylines. It verifies fidelity with MCE/rMCE and uses incident radiation as a fast diagnostic before heavier daylight and energy simulation.

View Project Details

ABMs in Architecture and Urban Design

A hands-on programme for translating human-space questions into explicit agent-based models. Forty-two participants used Rhino, Grasshopper, and Python to develop and interpret twelve architecture and urban-design projects.

View Bootcamp Archive

prinTerra

An academic system study combining mobile robotic 3D printing, local earth, parametric toolpaths, sensing, and passive form for arid environments.
